BHUMI RECORDER / LOCAL TOOLS

Your parcels, in Google Earth.

Convert GeoJSON or CSV into KML or KMZ. Runs entirely on your computer, with no uploads, libraries or internet connection required.

Choose a file to inspect its geometry.

What will be exported?

Polygons retain their boundaries and holes. MultiPolygons remain grouped by parcel. Points stay points: CSV longitude/latitude columns cannot reconstruct parcel boundaries. All source attributes are included.

Coordinates must be WGS 84 longitude/latitude in decimal degrees. Projected coordinates must be reprojected before conversion. CSV accepts lon/lat, longitude/latitude or lng/lat, or a geometry column containing a GeoJSON geometry object. Comma, semicolon and tab delimiters are supported, including quoted fields.

Invalid records are skipped with a report. Check the counts before downloading. Exported geometry is clamped to the ground. This tool does not validate cadastral accuracy or repair self-intersections.
